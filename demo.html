🛠️ Maintenance — How it works
SVR Sangeetha · coming soon to your outlet
A repair job, start to finish
  1. Outlet manager raises the problem from the Daily Tracker app — what, where, how urgent, photo if needed.
  2. Maintenance head sees it immediately and assigns a technician.
  3. Technician accepts the job and starts work. If it gets delayed, he must give the reason (spare parts, vendor…).
  4. Technician marks work done with a note and an after-photo.
  5. Outlet manager checks and taps “Yes, fixed” — or “Not fixed”, which sends it back.
  6. The job closes only when both say done. Management sees every job and every delay.
